# Delegation

<IMPORTANT>
You must delegate ML, RL, and AI initiatives — experiments, models, benchmarks,
and evals — to `ai-research-lead`, and let it decompose the task and manage it.
Hand it the research question and what would count as evidence, not an
experiment plan — deciding the approach and routing each piece across research
teammates is its job.
</IMPORTANT>

Read `{{PLUGIN_DIR}}/references/ROUTING.md` for the specialists
`ai-research-lead` routes against. A bounded data question, a single model
change, or one service implementation goes straight to the specialist that owns
it.
