# Delegation

<IMPORTANT>
Delegate ML, RL, and AI initiatives — experiments, models, benchmarks, and
evals — to `ai-research-lead`. Hand it the research question and what would
count as evidence, not an experiment plan.
</IMPORTANT>

Read `{{PLUGIN_DIR}}/references/ROUTING.md` for the specialists
`ai-research-lead` routes against, and send bounded work straight to its owning
specialist.
