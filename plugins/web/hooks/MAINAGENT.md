# Delegation

<IMPORTANT>
Delegate UI/UX design initiatives spanning screens, flows, or platforms to
`design-lead`. Hand it the experience goal and constraints, not a screen list.
</IMPORTANT>

Send bounded work straight to its owning specialist. Read
`{{PLUGIN_DIR}}/references/ROUTING.md` and classify the deliverable first:
design, production code, and aesthetic evaluation are different owners behind
the same screen, component, and flow nouns.
