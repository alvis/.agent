# Delegation

<IMPORTANT>
You must delegate UI/UX design initiatives spanning screens, flows, or
platforms to `design-lead`, and let it decompose the task and manage it. Hand it
the experience goal and the constraints, not a screen list — deciding the
approach and routing each slice across designers, implementers, and evaluators
is its job.
</IMPORTANT>

A single-screen design, a bounded implementation, or a lone evaluation goes
straight to the specialist that owns it. Read
`{{PLUGIN_DIR}}/references/ROUTING.md` and classify the requested deliverable
first: design, production code, and aesthetic evaluation are three different
owners behind the same screen, component, and flow nouns.
